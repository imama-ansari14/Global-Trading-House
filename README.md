# 🌐 Global Trading House

---

## ✨ Overview

**Global Trading House** is a modern business website developed for a US-based wholesale inventory company that sources bulk inventory from major US retailers and supplies buyers internationally.

The website was designed to establish a strong digital presence for the company, clearly present its wholesale inventory categories, communicate its sourcing model, and make it easy for potential buyers to get in touch.

This project was developed as a **freelance client project**.

## 🎯 Project Highlights

- Modern, professional wholesale business website
- Responsive design across desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Product-focused inventory presentation
- Dedicated sections for wholesale categories
- Clear calls-to-action for potential buyers
- Professional company presentation and brand positioning

---

## 📦 Inventory Categories

The website presents wholesale inventory across multiple categories, including:

- 👟 Shoes
- 👕 Clothing
- 💄 Cosmetics
- 👜 Bags
- 🔧 Power Tools

The structure is designed to allow additional inventory categories to be introduced as the business expands.

---

## 🌍 Business Focus

Global Trading House sources bulk inventory from leading US retailers and connects that inventory with resellers, distributors, and buyers internationally.

The website communicates the company's focus on:

- Bulk wholesale inventory
- US-sourced products
- Transparent inventory information
- Wholesale opportunities
- International buyers
- Direct business inquiries
